// 9 SYSTEM GATEWAYS

WHERE CAN I ENTER THE SYSTEM?

Every node in the ecosystem can enter the DigiSynq system. Whether you have a resource, need a capability, or face a constraint — we measure the situation, connect complementary nodes, and determine what may be ready for deployment. Different starting points. One connected system.

GATEWAY 01

I HAVE A PROJECT

What you have or need: A project that needs to move forward.
What we measure: Missing talent, facilities, equipment, capital, technology, distribution or audience connections.
What we connect: Creative, technical, facility, equipment and capital nodes.
What can be deployed: A coordinated execution pathway to move the project into action.

GATEWAY 02

I HAVE TALENT

What you have or need: Specialist capability or guild craft.
What we measure: Capabilities, availability and fit.
What we connect: Creators, performers and technicians with relevant projects.
What can be deployed: Verified talent attachments to active opportunities.

GATEWAY 03

I HAVE A FACILITY

What you have or need: Stages, studios, venues or locations with open capacity.
What we measure: Available capacity, timing and requirements.
What we connect: Stages, studios and venues with suitable production demand.
What can be deployed: Activated facility bookings and calendar optimization.

GATEWAY 04

I HAVE EQUIPMENT

What you have or need: Production equipment and technical capacity.
What we measure: Availability, technical fit and deployment conditions.
What we connect: Production gear and technical resources with relevant shoots.
What can be deployed: Fleet liquidity and active gear deployment.

GATEWAY 05

I HAVE IP / CONTENT

What you have or need: Scripts, formats, catalogues or story assets.
What we measure: Development, production, audience and distribution pathways.
What we connect: Content with the development, production and distribution resources required.
What can be deployed: New commercial value unlocked from content assets.

GATEWAY 06

I HAVE CAPITAL

What you have or need: Capital relationships seeking structured activity.
What we measure: Relevant opportunities, requirements and commercial structures.
What we connect: Capital relationships with suitable, risk-managed activity.
What can be deployed: Coordinated commercial alignment. (DigiSynq is not a bank, lender or fund).

GATEWAY 07

I NEED DISTRIBUTION

What you have or need: A route to market for developing or completed content.
What we measure: Relevant market, window, territory, audience and pathway.
What we connect: Content with appropriate theatrical, streaming, FAST and licensing partners.
What can be deployed: Target market release and syndication rollout.

GATEWAY 08

I HAVE AN AUDIENCE

What you have or need: Community, fandom or demand signals.
What we measure: Audience signals, behavior and demand.
What we connect: Communities with relevant content, experiences and commercial opportunities.
What can be deployed: Targeted audience alignment and event activation.

GATEWAY 09

I HAVE A BOTTLENECK

What you have or need: An opportunity blocked by something missing.
What we measure: What is missing, underused or at risk.
What we connect: The specific missing node or resource required to unlock progress.
What can be deployed: Targeted resolution that moves the opportunity forward.
