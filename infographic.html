Primary unit locked with pre-matched standby and adaptive failover parameters embedded before principal photography begins.
05

04 — The Verification Protocol

Evidence-Based. Not Self-Declared.

Six independent layers of validation. Every capability in the network is proven, not claimed.

L1
Credential & Identity Verification
Government ID, industry union memberships, and professional guild certifications cross-referenced against national registries.
L2
Equipment Proficiency Testing
Camera body, lens system, and technical pipeline mastery validated through standardised practical assessments.
L3
Past Project Wrap Log Review
Objective call-sheet records, equipment handling logs, and budget adherence data from verified productions reviewed.
L4
Peer Collaboration Score
Director, DOP, gaffer, and PM confirmation of working relationship quality. Aggregated across multiple projects.
L5
Technical Portfolio Audit
Frame-level inspection of portfolio footage by independent technical reviewers assessing quality across relevant formats.
L6
Continuous Performance Monitoring
Ratings auto-updated post every project. Grades recalibrated as new equipment and formats are verified. Network trust stays current.
