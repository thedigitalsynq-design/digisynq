SECTION 01

Category creation & operating thesis

DigiSynq operates as the Orchestration Layer for the Global Entertainment Ecosystem. The entertainment industry is structurally characterized by asset bloat, opaque broker networks, disconnected creative pods, and unmonetized dark capacity. DigiSynq does not create physical studios or act as a traditional agency—it standardizes and operates the economic and logistical connections between them.

THE DIGISYNQ OPERATING CREED

"We believe the entertainment ecosystem already contains enormous talent, infrastructure, capital, content, technology, audiences and opportunity. Too much of it remains fragmented, underused, disconnected or invisible."

DigiSynq exists to find that value, connect it, coordinate it, make it more transparent, activate it and measure what it creates.

"Because nothing is truly waste when the right connection can unlock its value."

The Fundamental Law: Don't own the ecosystem. Operate the connections between it. (Existing Resources × Better Coordination = New Economic Value)

The 12-step core loop standard operating procedure

Master Execution Protocol // Immutable Loop

Every production slate, stage booking, and crew packaging mission executed under DigiSynq follows this rigorous 12-phase Standard Operating Procedure. Below are the operational actions, triggers, inputs, verification gates, failovers, and output artifacts for each phase:

PHASE 01 // DETECT Discovery SLA: < 2 Hours

Fragmentation & Leakage Detection

SYNQSCAN™ continuously ingests lot calendars, camera package maintenance logs, and production permits to locate unmonetized dark days, stalled slates, and workflow leakage.

Step Execution Sequence:
1. Ingest: Automated scanning of partner facility availability feeds and client challenge intake forms.
2. Quantify: Calculate baseline economic leakage (dark days × day rate + crew idle cost).
3. Index: Generate unique Opportunity Tracking Code (OTC-####) and assign dedicated Lead Orchestrator.
Trigger Protocol
Stage schedule gap > 3 days or client challenge intake form.
Failover Protocol
If API unavailable, trigger direct telephone verification with lot GM within 60 mins.
Verification Gate
Double confirmation of unbooked dates and technical lot access parameters.
Output Artifact
Opportunity Intake Dossier (OID) with SHA-256 validation hash.
PHASE 02 // MAP Indexing SLA: < 4 Hours

Resource & Relational Graph Query

The system queries the DigiSynq Resource Graph to find certified craftspeople, soundstages, and optical packages matched to the project’s specific geographic and technical requirements.

Step Execution Sequence:
1. Query: Filter nodes across geographic radius, acoustic STC specs, and camera format requirements.
2. Weight: Score candidate compatibility based on historical collaboration graph velocity and wrap success.
3. Rank: Produce 3 calibrated candidate packages with transparent rate-card benchmarks.

Verification of guild status, past release credits, and equipment serial numbers.
Output Artifact

Capability Vetting & Technical Calibration

Rigorous bench QC verifies camera sensor collimation, lens flange focal depth, grip rigging safety certs, and technician rate card calibration prior to formal attachment.
