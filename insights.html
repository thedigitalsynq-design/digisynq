The verification stack

Six layers between mandate and wrap.

Every unit that touches a project passes the same stack — nothing moves on trust alone.

badge

L1 — identity & pedigree

Verified identity, filmography, and references against the registry.

account_balance_wallet

L2 — financial standing

Clean payment history and credit score indexed from ledger records.

gavel

L3 — legal & compliance

Contracts, licenses, and clearances checked before any unit is locked.

shield

L4 — risk profile

Historical slip rate and failure pattern scored against project type.

link

L5 — network coupling

Past collaboration graph read — units that have run clean together are rated higher.

monitoring

L6 — live performance

Real-time wrap data, attendance, and slippage re-score the unit continuously.

Failover plans

Plan A, B, and C — locked before day one.

Every schedule reserves alternate capacity so a slip never becomes a stall.

01

Mandate signed and indexed in the registry.

02

Units matched from verified capacity; failover slots reserved.

03

All six verification layers pass on every locked unit.

04

Wrap log written; intelligence loop updates the graph.

01

A locked unit slips — the graph flags it within hours, not weeks.

02

Reserved failover unit is activated from the pre-negotiated slot.

03

Schedule re-balances around the swap; dates hold where possible.

04

The slip is logged — the unit’s risk score updates for next time.

01

Failover slot also unavailable — the graph broadcasts to the open registry.

02

Multiple verified alternatives bid in hours; no unit is forced.

03

Project owner approves the best match from the shortlist.

04

Execution continues; plan C becomes the new plan A for the remainder.
