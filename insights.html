Insights • Industry in Numbers — Dummy Data

Entertainment is 68% fragmented.

Stages, talent, footage and audiences already exist. The value leaks in the gaps between them. Below are illustrative numbers — directionally right, not audited — to show where the network finds the loss.

42% idle capacity 38% hidden talent 71% single-use content 29% leakage

01 — At a Glance

Four places the value disappears.

42%
Capacity idle

Studio stages & gear sit idle 14.3 days / month on average. Peak vs. off-peak gap is 3.1×.

38%
Talent unseen

Verified technicians are available but not discovered — profiled but not matched to live briefs.

71%
Single-use content

Productions generate 28–46 assets — only 1.4 paths used. Rest dies after release.

29%
Budget leakage

Coordination friction eats ₹18–26L per project — re-booking, re-briefing, re-shoots.
