Business Model

We earn when the ecosystem
creates value.

DigiSynq does not need to own every asset, employ every specialist or control every transaction. We coordinate the ecosystem and participate when measurable value is created.

Measurable Impact

Where the value comes from

Higher utilization

Turning unbooked studio days and idle equipment benches into paid production days.

Faster coordination

Shrinking packaging and procurement cycles from months into days.

Lower friction

Eliminating arbitrary middleman markups, agency conflicts, and broker rent-seeking.

Better visibility

Providing real-time insight into true market rates, availability, and delivery status.

More opportunities

Connecting regional talent, indie producers, and shelved IP with national platforms.

Less idle capacity

Transforming carrying cost liabilities into active cash flow for facilities and vendors.

Transparent settlement

Milestone escrow governance ensuring artists, crew, and vendors are paid without delay or default.

Fee Architecture

Transparent revenue models.

Our business model is directly aligned with participant success. We succeed only when transactions and slates execute smoothly:

STREAM 01

Transaction Coordination Fees

Small percentage fee upon successful talent placement, facility booking, and gear package deployment.

STREAM 02

Project Coordination Fees

Managing workflow governance, daily DIT checksum tracking, and inter-stakeholder milestone sign-offs.

STREAM 03

Capacity Activation Fees

Success fee shared with studio lots when unbooked gap dates are successfully filled with paid productions.

STREAM 04

IP Packaging & Licensing

Participation fee when dormant scripts, books, or format rights are successfully optioned or adapted.

STREAM 05

Distribution Facilitation

Commission upon closing theatrical exhibition deals, OTT acquisition licenses, or foreign sales syndication.

STREAM 06

Cinema Radar & Intelligence

Pre-release threat mitigation, piracy interception, and aggregated audience intelligence benchmarks.

THE NETWORK EFFECT

Every completed cycle makes the network more valuable.

More participants → More visible capacity → Better matching → Faster coordination → Lower friction → Compounding value for the whole ecosystem.
