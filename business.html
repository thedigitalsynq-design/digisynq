Business Model

We monetize coordination, not ownership.

The assets already exist. DIGISYNQ creates value by connecting them — and captures a share of the value it helps create, through clearly defined services.

01 — How Value Moves

From project to captured value.

01

Projects

02 — How We Capture Value

Legitimate services, transparent economics.

Each revenue path is tied to work DIGISYNQ actually performs. No ownership of talent, IP or infrastructure — value is earned through coordination.

Coordination

Connection fees

For sourcing, matching and synchronizing the right talent, facilities, creators and partners around a project.

Execution

Deployment fees

For clearly scoped production support, workshop orchestration and operational audits.

Activation

Content extension

For packaging, distributing and monetizing existing content across new platforms and paths.

Utilization

Capacity sharing

For putting idle studios, equipment and crew to work at higher utilization.

Intelligence

Network insight

For structured access to what the network is learning — supply, demand and match patterns.

Principle

Asset-light

We never monetize ownership of the underlying asset. We monetize the connection that made it work.
