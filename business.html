Market Thesis

Cinema Is Distributed. Production Capacity Isn't.

Talent, equipment, studios, post-production and services exist across thousands of fragmented businesses and individuals. DIGISYNQ creates the coordination layer between them.

The Asset-Light Shift

For decades, production required renting giant, consolidated, monolithic soundstage lots. Today, top cameras, high-performance DIT suites, and virtual LED stages are owned by individuals and boutique partners scattered across hubs.

By indexing this distributed capacity into an active graph, DIGISYNQ allows producers to spin up virtual studio infrastructure on-demand with zero capital overhead.

THE NETWORK EFFECT

Compounding Value

Every project executed on the network registers verified wrap evidence, reliability logs, and compatibility data. As more projects run, our matching algorithm gets faster and failovers get tighter.
