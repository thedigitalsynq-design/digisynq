Market & revenue

Business model & market thesis.

An open explanation of how DIGISYNQ avoids economic leakage and maximizes outcome — an asset-light model that coordinates value instead of owning it.

Market thesis

Cinema is distributed. Production capacity isn't.

Talent, equipment, studios, post-production and services exist across thousands of fragmented businesses and individuals. DIGISYNQ creates the coordination layer between them.

The asset-light model

DIGISYNQ is not a production house, rental company, staffing agency, or marketing agency. It connects and coordinates the ecosystem — connecting existing resources instead of unnecessarily owning them.

By indexing distributed capacity into an active graph, DIGISYNQ allows producers to spin up virtual studio infrastructure on-demand with zero capital overhead.

The network effect

Compounding value

Every project executed on the network registers verified wrap evidence, reliability logs, and compatibility data. As more projects run, matching becomes faster and failovers grow tighter.
