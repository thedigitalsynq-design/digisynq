THE DIGISYNQ PROFESSIONAL ID.

Every technician, director, and facility gets a unique verified identity. We eliminate nepotism in favor of genuine merit and verified track records.

GRADE = Capability

Demonstrated technical depth, mastery of advanced camera rigs, DI color science, or complex production design.

RATING = Performance

Verified wrap feedback: on-time arrival, collaborative attitude, schedule discipline, and budget adherence.

Example: A Grade B / Rating 4.9 professional is highly reliable and disciplined while expanding technical depth. A Grade A / Rating 3.7 professional is technically brilliant but requires strict operational oversight.
