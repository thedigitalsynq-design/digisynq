Why DIGISYNQ

Production capacity is vast.
But it is deeply fragmented.

Cinema doesn't lack talent, high-end cameras, soundstages, or knowledge. The fundamental problem is coordination friction: capacity remains trapped in closed personal networks, calendar gaps sit unmonetized, and producers waste weeks negotiating with disjointed vendors.

The result is economic leakage — value that exists in the ecosystem but never reaches a screen: idle gear, empty stages, unavailable talent, and rework from avoidable failure.

“Cinema loses value not from a lack of creative capability, but from the friction of fragmented coordination.”
Talent → dispersed & inflexible
Hardware → sitting idle in cases
Soundstages → unbooked downtime
Budgets → lost in subcontract leaks
Failovers → single point of failure
Post-wrap → data & learnings evaporate

The mandate

Synchronized unit
Step 1: ingestion
Understands requirements

Parses the technical spec: low-light sensitivity, Cooke anamorphic look, ACES HDR delivery.

Step 2: graph match
Maps verified capacity

Cross-references calendar availability for Grade A DOPs and idle ARRI packages in Bengaluru.

Step 3: unit compose
Assembles right-sized unit

Locks pre-negotiated off-peak rate for stage 02 and pairs with verified DIT color pipeline.

Step 4: resilience
Locks Plan A / B / C
