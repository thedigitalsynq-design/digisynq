The Industry Problem

Production Capacity Is Vast.
But It Is Deeply Fragmented.

Cinema doesn't lack talent, high-end cameras, soundstages, or knowledge. The fundamental problem is coordination friction: capacity remains trapped in closed personal networks, calendar gaps sit unmonetized, and producers waste weeks negotiating disjointed vendors.

“Cinema loses value not from a lack of creative capability, but from the friction of fragmented coordination.”
Talent → Dispersed & Inflexible
Hardware → Sitting Idle in Cases
Soundstages → Unbooked Downtime
Budgets → Lost in Subcontract Leaks
Failovers → Single Point of Failure
Post-Wrap → Data & Learnings Evaporate
