Platform Architecture

One ecosystem.
One coordination layer.

DigiSynq helps entertainment participants discover what is available, find what is needed, coordinate the moving parts, execute with greater transparency and learn from every completed cycle.

OPERATING DOCTRINE
From fragmented activity to coordinated action:
Discover → Match → Coordinate → Secure → Execute → Settle → Learn
Core Capabilities

The 5 platform engines.

Human-friendly categories powered by sophisticated technology underneath:

ENGINE 01 Capacity Activation

RESOURCE ENGINE

Find available capacity, facilities, equipment and services.

Aggregates unbooked soundstage dark days, calibrated prime lens benches, grip packages, and location windows. Turns dead time into immediate production output.

Underlying Tech: SYNQSCAN™ leakage scanner & SYNQPOOL™ dark-capacity clearing.
ENGINE 02 Talent Verification

PEOPLE ENGINE

Find the right talent and expertise at the right time.

Neutral pairing for directors, cinematographers, writers, actors, and guild artisans. Indexes verified credit ledgers and schedule availability matrices with zero broker conflicts.

Underlying Tech: SYNQMAP™ relational capability graph & SYNQLINK™ team attachment.
ENGINE 03 IP Activation

IP ENGINE

Discover, package, adapt and connect dormant or active IP.

Catalogs shelved scripts, published books, regional formats, and music rights. Packages intellectual property with aligned showrunners and distribution windows.

Underlying Tech: Rights verification, format adapters, and catalog packaging.
ENGINE 04 Ecosystem Analytics

INTELLIGENCE ENGINE

Understand audiences, markets, opportunities and risks.

Evaluates viewer appetite, regional distribution demand, and pre-release sentiment risks. Informs smarter production and release decisions before capital is locked.

Underlying Tech: SYNQINTEL™ multi-production analytics & Cinema Radar sentiment shield.
ENGINE 05 Trust & Settlement

VALUE ENGINE

Track agreements, milestones, revenue, rights and settlements.

Governs milestone draws, verified delivery sign-offs, and transparent escrow releases. Eliminates invoice lag and payment defaults so artists and vendors are protected.

Underlying Tech: SYNQVAULT™ milestone escrow governance & SYNQTRACK™ live delivery verification.
