Section 01 // Why We Exist

The Founding Thesis

The Indian entertainment economy generates billions in gross output annually — but the mechanisms connecting talent, capital, and production capacity are still largely informal, relationship-dependent, and opaque. Deals break down not because the industry lacks the resources to make them, but because there is no shared operating infrastructure for trust.

DIGISYNQ was built to be that infrastructure. Not an agency. Not a production house. Not a fund. An operating network — a neutral connective tissue that routes the right assets to the right opportunities at the right moment, with verified milestone accountability built in.
