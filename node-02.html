What does DigiSynq connect?

// THE PHILOSOPHY OF THE NODE

EVERYTHING CAN BE A NODE. DIGISYNQ SYNCHRONIZES THEM.

A person is a node. A stage is a node. A screenplay is a node. An archival catalogue is a node. A matched anamorphic prime set is a node. An audience is a node. Capital is a node. Even an unscheduled 10-day soundstage window is a node. DigiSynq does not own these assets; we provide the intelligent, asset-light coordination layer that maps them, aligns their incentives, and synchronizes them on demand.
