Frequently Asked Questions

How onboarding, deal structures, and escrow protections work on the DIGISYNQ network.

Is it free to enter the DIGISYNQ network?

Yes. There are zero upfront registration or subscription fees for creative talent, producers, or studio facilities. DIGISYNQ earns a transparent coordination fee only when verified deals close and milestone payments are delivered.

How does milestone escrow protect our production funds?

All production capital is locked in multi-sig milestone escrow accounts. Funds are only disbursed when neutral stage-gate auditors verify that delivery milestones (script lock, shoot wrap, post delivery) have been fully met.

How fast can a dark soundstage or talent window be booked?

Once a brief is matched with an available facility or talent pod, standardized digital deal memos allow contract execution and escrow funding to be finalized within 48 to 72 hours.
