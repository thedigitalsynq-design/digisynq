CAPITAL & ESCROW:
RADICAL CERTAINTY.

Capital avoids entertainment not because margins are low, but because coordination risk is high. DigiSynq introduces institutional certainty through SYNQVAULT™ milestone escrow, automated completion verification, and non-dilutive slate funding.

SYNQVAULT™ ESCROW

Milestone Disbursal

Production capital is locked in independent multi-sig escrow accounts. Funds only release when physical footage and sound assets are verified.

RISK MITIGATION

Completion Bonds & Insurance

Institutional underwriting covering weather delays, principal talent illness, optical sensor damage, and delivery default.

CHAIN-OF-TITLE

Uncompromising Legal Defense

Clean script registries, verified author releases, and guild-compliant SAG/CINTAA contracts built into every deal sheet.

SLATE INVESTORS

Portfolio Risk Diversification

Allocating capital across multiple coordinated productions amortizes risk while participating in outsized theatrical and OTT upside.

ZERO BROKER DRAG

No Layered Commission Cuts

Direct platform coordination cuts out opaque intermediaries and percentage finders who extract capital without contributing value.

AUDIT TRAIL

Real-Time Financial Dashboard

Lenders and investors track petty cash burn, vendor invoices, and wrap completion in an immutable digital ledger.

// ECONOMIC ARCHITECTURE

We Earn When The Ecosystem Creates Value

DigiSynq does not need to own every asset, employ every specialist, or monopolize transactions. We coordinate the ecosystem and participate only when measurable value is created:

STREAM 01

Transaction Coordination

Transparent success-based coordination fee upon verified placement of talent, soundstages, and camera packages.

STREAM 02

Milestone Escrow Governance

SYNQVAULT™ multi-signature escrow verification ensuring non-delayed milestone payouts and proof-of-work clearance.

STREAM 03

Dark Capacity Activation

Performance yield shared with studio facilities when unbooked dark days are filled with commercial or digital shoots.

STREAM 04

IP Packaging & Pre-Sales

Participation fee upon pairing dormant literary/script IP with attached director pods and guaranteed streaming pre-sales.

// THE 7 GOVERNANCE RULES

Clear Expectations Replace Ambiguity

Rule 01 // Who does what: Explicit scope definitions across crew, producers, and vendors.
Rule 02 // When it happens: Locked production milestones tied to realistic calendars.
Rule 03 // What is required: Objective technical specs, acoustic ratings, and format standards.
Rule 04 // What is owed: Transparent compensation, rate cards, and bonus waterfalls.
Rule 05 // If things change: Pre-agreed contingency mechanisms for weather, delays, and reshoots.
Rule 06 // When money moves: Milestone escrow payouts triggered upon verified proof of work.
Rule 07 // Role-Based Visibility: Sensitive creative IP and budgets remain protected while schedule status is universally clear.
