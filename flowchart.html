Operating Protocol

Trust shouldn't depend on
who you know.

Entertainment works through thousands of relationships. DigiSynq turns those relationships into clearer operating rules.

THE OPERATING RULES

Clear expectations replace ambiguity

RULE 01

Who does what.

Explicit scope definitions across crew, producers, and vendors.

RULE 02

When it happens.

Locked production milestones tied to realistic schedules.

RULE 03

What is required.

Objective technical specs, acoustic ratings, and format standards.

RULE 04

What is owed.

Transparent compensation, rate cards, and bonus waterfalls.

RULE 05

What happens if something changes.

Pre-agreed contingency mechanisms for weather, delays, and reshoots.

RULE 06

When money moves.

Milestone escrow payouts triggered upon verified proof of work.

RULE 07

Who can see what.

Role-based visibility ensures sensitive creative IP and budgets remain protected while status is clear.

SYNQVAULT™ ESCROW GOVERNANCE

Programmable milestone releases replace chasing invoices

Production funds are held under transparent, multi-signature custody. When stage wrap, rough cut, or format delivery is certified, payouts disburse without human friction or arbitrary holding delays.
