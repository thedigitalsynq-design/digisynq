VERIFIED ECOSYSTEM • TAG TAXONOMY

The verified network & capability taxonomy.

Every participant is structurally classified, peer-verified, and contextually positioned — not by reputation alone, but by functional precision. The TAG system is the backbone of the DIGISYNQ matching engine.

Functional classification

The TAG 1–10 taxonomy.

Ten mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive functional domains. Functional capability — not status, not seniority, not popularity.

Core architectural principle: TAG is a functional classification — not a value judgment. TAG 10 is not superior to TAG 01; each defines specialized capability and ecosystem positioning.

TAG 01
Cinematography & camera

DPs, camera operators, gaffers, specialized optical and lighting technicians. Practitioners who translate narrative intent into visual language through optics, exposure, and movement.

TAG 02
Narrative architecture

Screenwriters, script doctors, story researchers, worldbuilders and dramaturgs. The architects of story structure — from premise to final polish across features, series, and formats.

TAG 03
Production design

Art directors, set dressers, costume designers, practical FX and prop masters. Physical world-builders who translate a script's setting and tone into tangible visual environments.

TAG 04
VFX & virtual production

LED volume supervisors, Unreal artists, compositors, 3D generalists, CGI leads. The visual effects and virtual production specialists extending practical reality into the digital domain.

TAG 05
Audio & sound design

Production sound mixers, foley artists, composers, spatial audio engineers. Practitioners who construct the sonic world — from set recording to full theatrical delivery.

TAG 06
Editorial & post craft

Picture editors, colorists, finishing artists, mastering and delivery engineers. The post-production specialists who shape final narrative rhythm, visual grade, and delivery output.

TAG 07
Directorial & creative vision

Directors, second-unit leads, showrunners, narrative visual consultants. Practitioners responsible for unified creative vision and on-set execution of the story's intent.

TAG 08
Production & logistics

Line producers, unit production managers, location managers, safety controllers. The operational infrastructure of production — scheduling, budgeting, logistics, and execution control.

TAG 09
Cultural & brand strategy

Brand integration architects, cultural advisors, narrative IP strategists. Specialists bridging commercial investment and authentic cultural integration within creative work.

TAG 10
Audience & distribution

Platform synchronizers, community orchestrators, theatrical and streaming curators. Practitioners who connect finished work to the audiences and distribution ecosystems that amplify its value.

Verification standard

Trust is structural, not reputational.

Trust within DIGISYNQ is built through evidence — not anecdote, social capital, or industry relationships.

Credential verification

All screen credits, studio associations, and technical certifications are cross-referenced with third-party production records and peer-verified by existing network participants before approval.

Execution telemetry

Budget adherence ratios, on-time wrap records, and production discipline markers are recorded post-collaboration and contribute to long-term network standing.

Feedback loops

Post-wrap mutual evaluations from verified project collaborators enrich each participant's contextual trust score — making the network smarter with every completed project.

Dispute arbitration

Standardized milestone records and documented delivery receipts provide clear, neutral arbitration infrastructure when expectations diverge during or after production.
