COMMAND 01: CREATE — Production, Talent & Advanced Execution

The 30-Day Principal Sprint

Rapid feature photography executed with pre-locked department pods, daily DIT checksum verification, and zero schedule drift under fixed-yield charters.

MISSION 02

The Shelved IP Revival

Packaging unproduced premium screenplays with attached guild artisan pods and milestone-governed multi-sig escrow financing.

MISSION 03

The High-Concept Adaptation

Translating bestselling regional literary IP and investigative podcast reporting into premium multi-episode episodic series formats.

COMMAND 02: ACTIVATE — Idle Capacity & Dormant Assets

MISSION 04

The Soundstage Dark-Day Rescue

Monetizing unbooked 5-to-15 day soundstage intervals between major studio builds with high-yield virtual production and commercial shoots.

MISSION 05

The Optical Kit Commercial Blitz
