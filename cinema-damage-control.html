01 — The reality

Every film emits a live digital current.

Without orchestration, digital currents turn volatile.

A film's digital life begins the moment the first teaser frame is uploaded and ends long after the OTT run closes. Between those two points, the conversation is live, relentless, and often volatile.

  • A single trailer frame can ignite algorithmic backlash within hours.
  • An isolated critic review can become an industry-wide opening weekend narrative.
  • A rogue creator's commentary can derail months of promotional momentum.
  • Manufactured rumour can reach the box office tracking agencies before the facts do.
  • A casting controversy can harden into permanent audience avoidance behaviour.

DIGISYNQ isolates narrative turbulence in real time, diagnoses root cultural signals, and executes calibrated counter-measures to safeguard commercial momentum.
