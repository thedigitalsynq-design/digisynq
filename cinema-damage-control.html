Section 02 // Architecture

The PCR Framework

Coordinated intervention phases covering the full release lifecycle. Pre-Release establishes the baseline. Crisis intercepts in real-time. Recovery compounds what survives.

01 // PRE-RELEASE SHIELD

Pre-Release

Sentinel-mode monitoring from 30 days out. Identify, calibrate, and neutralize before narrative damage compounds.

  • Trailer sentiment velocity analysis across comment clusters
  • Coordinated hashtag seeding across aligned creator networks
  • Advance critic and verified reviewer pipeline synchronization
  • BookMyShow and Paytm demand tracking across cities
  • Dark social monitoring: private Reddit threads and Telegram groups
02 // CRISIS DEFLECTION

Crisis

Immediate-response infrastructure when a narrative threat becomes active. Every minute without response compounds the damage.

  • Real-time review bomb detection vs. organic feedback
  • Counter-narrative rapid deployment: cast activations & BTS content
  • Platform escalation protocols on IMDb, Google & BookMyShow
  • Exhibitor communication loops to protect morning screen count
  • Distributor PR desk alignment across regional release markets
03 // RECOVERY & COMPOUND

Recovery

Post-crisis stabilization and upside capture. Convert a corrected narrative into a compounding second-week story.

  • Second-week audience expansion narrative repositioning
  • Word-of-mouth amplification engine with verified testimonials
  • OTT platform rights negotiation leverage via sentiment data
  • Merchandise and ancillary window narrative protection
  • Post-mortem intelligence fed back into the next release shield
Section 03 // Operational Process

Live Telemetry Radar Loop

A closed-loop process that continuously monitors, intercepts, counters, and compounds — like a control tower that never sleeps.

01

SCAN — Continuous Monitoring

24/7 sentiment tracking across YouTube trailer comments, Instagram Reels, Facebook fan communities, Reddit discussion megathreads, X (Twitter), BookMyShow advance bookings, and regional Telegram groups. Signal frequency: every 4 hours.

02

ISOLATE — Threat Classification

Distinguish between organic negative sentiment and coordinated review bombing. Classify threat severity (Low, Medium, Critical) and assign rapid response protocols.

03

COUNTER — Rapid Response Deployment

Activate pre-prepared counter-narrative assets: verified cast content, curated audience testimonials, and behind-the-scenes clarifications within 6 hours.

04

PROTECT — Exhibitor and Platform Shield

Communicate corrected sentiment signals directly to exhibitor chains to prevent premature screen reductions. Coordinate review integrity flags on manipulation evidence.

05

TELEMETRY — Post-Response Analysis

Measure counter-narrative asset performance. Feed all telemetry data back into the pre-release shield model for the next film in the production slate.
